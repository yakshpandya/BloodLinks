The Process of Blood Donation

Everything you need to know before, during, and after your donation.

How Blood Donation Works

Donating blood is a simple, safe, and life-saving act. The entire process takes about 45–60 minutes — but the impact lasts a lifetime for the person who receives it. One donation can save up to 3 lives.

Step-by-Step Process

1

Registration

Fill out a donor registration form with your personal details including name, age, address, and blood group. You'll receive a donor ID for future reference.

2

Health Screening

A trained healthcare professional will check your blood pressure, pulse, temperature, hemoglobin level, and a few basic medical questions to ensure you're eligible to donate.

3

The Donation

You'll sit comfortably while approximately 450ml of blood is drawn from your arm through a sterile needle. The process takes only 8–12 minutes. You'll feel minimal discomfort.

4

Rest & Refreshments

After donating, rest for 10–15 minutes. Juice, biscuits, or snacks are provided. You should avoid strenuous activity for the rest of the day.

5

Blood Processing

Your donated blood is sent to a lab where it's tested for infectious diseases and separated into components (red cells, platelets, plasma) to help multiple patients.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 18 to 65 years
  • Weight: Minimum 45 kg
  • Hemoglobin: At least 12.5 g/dL
  • No fever, cold, or active infection in the last 2 weeks
  • No donation in the last 56 days (3 months for women)
  • Not on any antibiotic or blood-thinning medication
  • No tattoo or piercing in the last 6 months

Benefits of Donating Blood

  • Saves up to 3 lives per donation
  • Free health screening and blood group check
  • Reduces risk of heart disease and some cancers
  • Stimulates production of new red blood cells
  • Burns approximately 650 calories per donation
  • Emotional fulfilment — the joy of saving a life

Your body replenishes the donated plasma within 24 hours and the red blood cells within 4–6 weeks. You can donate whole blood every 56 days safely.
