Pre & Post Donation Tips

Prepare your body before donation and recover well afterward.

Before You Donate (Pre-Donation)

  • Eat a healthy meal — Have a nutritious low-fat meal at least 3 hours before donation
  • Stay hydrated — Drink at least 500ml of water or juice before donating
  • Sleep well — Get a full night's rest (7–8 hours) before your donation day
  • Avoid alcohol — Do not consume alcohol for at least 24 hours before donation
  • Avoid fatty foods — Fatty meals can affect blood tests and eligibility
  • Wear comfortable clothes — Wear a shirt with sleeves that roll up easily
  • Bring ID proof — Carry a valid government-issued ID to the donation centre
  • Inform about medications — Tell the health professional about any medications you take

Avoid donating blood if you have taken antibiotics in the last 48 hours, or blood-thinning medications in the last 72 hours. Consult the blood bank staff if unsure.

After You Donate (Post-Donation)

  • Rest for 15 minutes — Stay at the donation centre and rest before leaving
  • Drink plenty of fluids — Drink extra water, juice, or sports drinks for 24–48 hours
  • Eat iron-rich foods — Spinach, lentils, meat, and fortified cereals help replenish iron
  • Avoid strenuous exercise — Skip heavy gym workouts for the rest of the day
  • Keep the bandage on — Keep the pressure bandage on for at least 4 hours
  • Avoid alcohol for 24 hours — Alcohol can increase dehydration and dizziness
  • Don't smoke for 2 hours — Allow your blood pressure to stabilise first
  • If you feel dizzy — Sit or lie down immediately, raise your legs, and inform nearby staff

Your body replaces the donated plasma within 24 hours and fully replaces red blood cells within 4–6 weeks. You can donate whole blood safely every 56 days.
