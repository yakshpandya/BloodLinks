About BloodLinks

Our mission, vision, and the team behind India's leading blood donation network.

🩸

Connecting Donors with Those Who Need Blood

BloodLinks is a technology-driven platform built by Colossal Health Private Limited, dedicated to streamlining blood donation and blood requests across India. We believe that no one should die from lack of blood when a voluntary donor is just a click away.

Our Mission

To build India's most comprehensive, accessible and trusted blood donation ecosystem — connecting voluntary blood donors, blood banks, hospitals, and patients in real time through technology.

We aim to make blood available to every patient in need, at any time, from anywhere in India, by empowering a community of heroes who donate voluntarily.

Our Vision

A world where blood shortage is history and every patient in need of blood gets it within minutes — through a seamless, verified, and free platform connecting millions of donors and blood banks.
